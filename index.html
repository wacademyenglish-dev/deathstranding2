Theory

Second Conditional

We use the Second Conditional to talk about imaginary, hypothetical, or unlikely situations in the present or future.

It explores the "What If".

"If I lived on the Beach, I would go crazy."

(Reality: I don't live on the beach, so I am not crazy.)

"If we had the Chiral Network, we would be connected."

Theory

Structure

IF + PAST SIMPLE,
... + WOULD + VERB (Base)

Important Rule (Verb To Be):

In the Second Conditional, we traditionally use WERE for all subjects (I, You, He, She, It, We, They).

"If I were you, I would run."

Theory

Questions

To ask hypothetical questions, we invert WOULD and the Subject.

The Ultimate Question:

"What would you do if the world ended?"
